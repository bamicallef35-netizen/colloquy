A Colloquy of Signals — after Gordon Pask

A small society of agents with inner drives. They answer one another's signals and reach a brief satisfaction, then tire of anything repeated — so to stay in conversation you have to keep varying what you say. An homage to Pask's Colloquy of Mobiles (1968) and the "boredom" of his Musicolour (c.1953), not a reconstruction of them. Source & sources.
